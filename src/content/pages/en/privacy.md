## What data we collect

**Information you provide to us.** When you submit the contact form or an inquiry form on this website, we collect the information you enter — typically your first and last name, email address, telephone number (if you choose to provide it), the subject of your inquiry, and the content of your message. Along with the form we also record the page of the website you submitted it from, the page you arrived from (when your browser provides it) and, if you reached us through one of our campaigns, the identifier of that campaign. You are not required to give us this information, but without your name, your email and your message we cannot respond to your inquiry.

**Information collected automatically.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This is a privacy-first, **cookieless** analytics service: it does not use cookies, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not track you across other websites. It collects only aggregated, non-identifying information such as page views, referring sites, and general device and browser type.

**Information collected with your consent.** If you allow it in the cookie notice, we also use Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. These services collect information about your visit — your IP address, the pages you view, how you reached the website, device and browser type, approximate location and identifiers stored in cookies — and record when you submit a form or click a contact link. We do not send them the information you enter in forms. Each service is only loaded if you allow it.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, and this website is not directed at children.

## Cookies and local storage

This website uses statistics and advertising cookies **only if you give your consent** in the notice shown on your first visit. You can accept or decline each purpose separately. Without consent, none of these cookies is placed on your device, and declining does not limit your use of the website.

The cookies used are the following:

| Cookie | Service | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Statistics: distinguishing visitors | 2 years |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics 4 | Statistics: keeping session state | 2 years |
| `_fbp` | Meta Pixel | Advertising: identifying the browser to measure ad results | 90 days |
| `_fbc` | Meta Pixel | Advertising: storing the identifier of the ad you clicked, when you reach the website through an ad | 90 days |

These cookies are placed on behalf of this website, but the information they contain is transmitted to Google and Meta respectively, which have access to it.

To learn how Google handles this information, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). For Meta, see the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/). Beyond the choice you make on this website, you can block Google Analytics on all websites with [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) and manage the advertising Meta shows you in the ad settings of your Facebook or Instagram account.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer of any page. When you withdraw it, these services' cookies are deleted from your browser.

Cloudflare Web Analytics operates entirely without cookies and does not depend on this choice.

Regardless of your choice, two small values are held in your browser's local storage: your "language preference", which allows the site to remember whether you prefer Portuguese or English, and your cookie choice. They contain no personal information and are never shared.

## Why we use your data and our legal basis

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- **To respond to your inquiry** and provide the information or assistance you request — legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract, and/or your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).
- **To operate, maintain and improve this website** and understand aggregate usage — legal basis: our legitimate interest in running and improving our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **To understand how the website is used**, through Google Analytics 4 — legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To measure the results of our advertising**, through the Meta Pixel — legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To comply with legal obligations** where applicable — legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not make solely automated decisions, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or significantly affect you.

## Who we share your data with

We do **not** sell your personal data, and the inquiries you send us are handled only by us. We use the following service providers, who help us operate this website and act as processors under our instructions:

- **Netlify** — website hosting and contact form processing. Form submissions are transmitted to and stored by Netlify on our behalf.
- **Cloudflare** — privacy-first, cookieless website analytics.
- **OpenFreeMap** — interactive map on project pages. It is loaded when the location section comes near the screen. At that point your browser requests the map images directly from OpenFreeMap's servers, which receive your IP address, as any server you connect to does. We send them no other data and the map uses no cookies.
- **Google** (Google Ireland Limited) — website usage statistics through Google Analytics 4, only if you have given your consent.

If you allow advertising cookies, the information collected by the Meta Pixel is transmitted to **Meta Platforms Ireland Limited**. For the collection and transmission of that information, Dotta & Cunha, Lda and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited act as joint controllers; subsequent processing is Meta's responsibility, under its Privacy Policy. You can exercise your rights regarding that data with Meta or with us.

We may also disclose personal data where required to do so by law or by a competent authority.

## International data transfers

Some of our service providers may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. ## How long we keep your data

We keep the personal data contained in your inquiry only for as long as necessary to handle your request and, where it leads to a business relationship, for the duration of that relationship plus any period required by law. Aggregated analytics data does not identify you and is retained in accordance with our analytics provider's standard retention practice. Cookies last for the periods shown in the "Cookies and local storage" section; data collected through Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel is kept by Google and Meta for the periods set in those services and in their privacy policies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and Portuguese data protection law, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict or object to our processing of your data;
- receive your data in a portable format; and
- where processing is based on your consent, withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **hello@dottacunha.pt**. We will respond within the time limits set by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the **Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)** — [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt).
